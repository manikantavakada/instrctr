## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-velocity, forward-leaning visual language engineered for next-generation urban multimodal transportation. Merging the precision of high-frequency transit telemetry with the polish of consumer lifestyle technology, the aesthetic rejects dull enterprise conventions in favor of hyper-clean, optimistic digital-first ergonomics.

The personality balances computational authority with frictionless velocity. It evokes instant trust, fluid motion, and tactical clarity. Target users span high-density urban commuters, fleet dispatchers, micro-mobility riders, and autonomous transit consumers who demand split-second legibility under bright sunlight and quick ambient glances.

The design movement synthesizes **Modern High-Contrast Precision** with **Atmospheric Kinetic Glassmorphism**:
- Ultra-crisp porcelain structural planes paired against intense, deep indigo night modes.
- High-chroma dynamic accents indicating live routes, fleet pulses, and system throughput.
- Frosted micro-surfaces with ultra-thin luminance-bearing borders that separate telemetry layers without clutter.
- Tactile visual feedback tuned for instant state recognition across motion-heavy interfaces.

## Colors

The palette establishes an electrifying contrast ratio between deep foundational anchors, pure atmospheric surfaces, and hyper-kinetic telemetry hues.

### Primary Palette & Structural Foundations
- **Deep Velocity Navy (`#0B132B` & `#1C2541`)**: Serves as the primary typographic and high-contrast structural anchor. In light mode, it commands core text hierarchy, solid navigation rails, and primary touchpoints. In dark mode variants, it acts as the atmospheric void surface.
- **Porcelain Surface & Slate Tint (`#FFFFFF` & `#F8FAFC`)**: Canvas base foundation. Rejects murky grays for a sterile, surgical, high-luminance canvas that makes live map layers and dynamic metrics pop.
- **Subtle Surface Stroke (`#E2E8F0`)**: Architectural divider used for low-friction containment lines and border boundaries.

### Dynamic Accent Spectrum
- **Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**: The interactive heartbeat. Denotes primary action pulses, multimodal route selection, active telemetry nodes, and selected digital assets. Accompanied by **Aero Teal (`#0EA5E9`)** to provide functional contrast and gradient vector endpoints.
- **Cyber Lime / Traffic Signal Green (`#22C55E` & `#84CC16`)**: Mobility-status driver indicating operational greenlights, available scooters/EVs, rapid battery density, on-time schedules, and peak eco-routing.
- **Signal Warning & Rush Alert (`#F59E0B` & `#EF4444`)**: Real-time traffic blockages, route congestion, and fleet maintenance warnings.

Use Electric Cyan selectively to retain its communicative power—it should never be diluted on passive background fills, only on active vector points, indicators, and key touch targets.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the open geometry and confident posture of **Plus Jakarta Sans** with the structural, high-density legibility of **Inter** for data overlays, status pills, and system readouts.

- **Headlines & Display**: Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with deliberate negative letter spacing to communicate technical precision, speed, and modern architectural form.
- **Body Content**: Employs comfortable line heights that sustain legibility during movement or outdoor glare.
- **Labels, Telemetry & Status Readouts**: Inter provides neutral, uncompromised glyph recognition for numerical speeds, vehicle battery percentages, countdown timers, and ETAs.
- Numbers in route tracking and duration badges should always use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent optical jitter during real-time GPS coordinate streaming.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a fluid-first 12-column grid on desktop screens (1024px and above), shifting to an 8-column system on tablets (768px–1023px) and a high-efficiency 4-column column structure on mobile viewports (<768px).

### Layout Rules
- **Map & Canvas Parity**: Spatial experiences alternate between fixed-viewport full-screen map canvases (z-index 0) and layered floating UI modular cards (z-index 10+).
- **Rhythm**: Built on a strict 4px/8px modular scale. Component interiors enforce compact density (`space-sm` to `space-md`), preserving vertical view-canvas for spatial context.
- **Form-Factor Transitions**:
  - *Mobile*: 16px lateral safety margins (`margin-mobile`). Floating interaction sheets anchor to the bottom viewport with draggable snap points (peek, half-sheet, full modal).
  - *Desktop*: 48px lateral margins (`margin-desktop`). Multi-modal drawer overlays position docked to the left canvas, maintaining persistent visibility of urban transit paths.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a hybrid model of **Aero Tonal Layering** combined with **Atmospheric Ambient Glazes**. Depth is never heavy, dark, or dirty; it behaves like daylight passing through polarized synthetic glass.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: 
   - Pure structural background (`#F8FAFC`). Flat with no shadows. Map vectors and high-contrast lines sit directly at this depth.

2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Layer)**:
   - Surface: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border using `#E2E8F0`.
   - Shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 37, 65, 0.04), 0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 37, 65, 0.06)`. Tinted with deep velocity indigo instead of flat gray.

3. **Level 2 (Active Overlays & Floating Hubs)**:
   - Surface: Pure white or frosted translucent (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`) backed with a `16px` backdrop-blur filter.
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`.
   - Shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.08)`. Subtle chromatic cyan bleed signals live real-time connection.

4. **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Critical Telemetry Overrides)**:
   - Backdrop: Deep Indigo scrim (`rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.45)`) with `8px` blur.
   - Surface: `#FFFFFF`.
   - Shadow: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Level 2 (Rounded)** shape posture. This geometry bridges human-friendly mobility consumer design with the aerodynamic curvature of contemporary electric vehicles and urban infrastructure.

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem)**: Employed on utility elements, interactive inputs, nested pills, and small list items.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` / 1.0rem)**: Applied to trip summary cards, vehicle selection modules, routing containers, and navigation bars.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem)**: Reserved for primary bottom sheets, overarching modal cards, and floating hero panels.
- **Pill Form (Fully Rounded / 9999px)**: Strictly preserved for micro-tags, vehicle battery state capsules, transit mode toggles, and contextual floating action triggers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic**: Deep Velocity Indigo (`#1C2541`) background with crisp white typography. Hover triggers a shift toward `#0B132B` paired with an outer focus-ring of high-energy Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`).
- **Telemetry Action (Hero)**: Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) surface with Deep Velocity Navy typography (`#0B132B`, weight 700). Used exclusively for critical path interactions (e.g., "Confirm Ride", "Unlock Scooter").
- **Secondary Glass**: Translucent slate (`rgba(241, 245, 249, 0.8)`) with navy label and subtle `1px` border (`#CBD5E1`).
- **Icon Buttons**: Uniform `44px x 44px` minimum hit area with `rounded-md` (8px) or circular profiles, ensuring touch precision on uneven transit paths.

### Cards & Dynamic Mobility Sheets
- Built with high-luminance white (`#FFFFFF`), `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg`), and crisp border lines (`#E2E8F0`).
- Interactive selection states trigger a `2px` stroke border swap from neutral slate to active Electric Cyan or Aero Teal, accompanied by an ambient internal cyan tint (`rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.03)`).

### Transit Chips & Status Tags
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) modules featuring distinct visual modes:
  - *Fast Route / Open Green*: Surface of soft lime tint (`#ECFDF5`), text in Cyber Green (`#15803D`), accompanied by a pulsating `6px` live dot.
  - *Vehicle Battery*: Neutral slate backing (`#F1F5F9`) with integrated micro-progress bars indicating real-time discharge levels.
  - *Transit Filter*: Inactive chips use a plain border (`#E2E8F0`); active chips toggle into solid Indigo (`#1C2541`) with white text.

### Inputs & Destination Search
- Height: `52px` for primary destination fields to facilitate rapid finger tapping.
- Surface: `#F8FAFC` recessed background with an inset highlight stroke. Focus immediately transitions the input surface to `#FFFFFF` with an Electric Cyan glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)`).
- Left-adorned icon markers color-code origin (Cyber Lime dot) and destination (Electric Cyan pin).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom-built geometric indicators: Unchecked state uses a `1.5px` border of `#CBD5E1`.
- Checked state fills with Electric Cyan or Indigo, presenting a sharp, high-contrast white vector icon.

### Route & Telemetry Timeline Lists
- Clean vertical flow connected by a continuous `2px` kinetic track line. 
- Transit station stops represented by hollow circles that illuminate with Cyber Lime or Cyan once reached or active.
